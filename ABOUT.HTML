Here is the complete top 10 breakdown of the most powerful Pals in Palworld, ranked by their base combat stats, exclusive partner skills, and overall utility in endgame content. --- ## 👑 The Top 10 Most Powerful Pals ### 1. Jetragon (Dragon) * **Partner Skill:** *Aerial Missile* (Can be ridden as a flying mount. Can rapidly fire a missile launcher while mounted). * **Why it's #1:** It is the fastest mount in the entire game and boasts unmatched base Attack scaling. Its missiles stun lock bosses, making it the ultimate combat initiator. ### 2. Frostallion (Ice) * **Partner Skill:** *Ice Pegasus* (Can be ridden as a flying mount. Changes the player's attack type to Ice and enhances Ice attacks while mounted). * **Why it's elite:** It has massive defensive stats and its partner skill grants a whopping 50% damage boost to all Ice attacks, completely freezing tough Dragon-type enemies. ### 3. Paladius (Neutral) * **Partner Skill:** *Holy Knight of the Firmament* (Can be ridden as a mount. Enables triple-jump while mounted). * **Why it's elite:** The ultimate tank. It boasts the highest base Defense stat in Palworld and uses powerful AoE skills like *Paladin Spear* to clear crowds safely. ### 4. Necromus (Dark) * **Partner Skill:** *Dark Knight of the Abyss* (Can be ridden as a mount. Enables double-jump while mounted). * **Why it's elite:** The counterpart to Paladius, focusing entirely on high offensive output. Its base physical attack stat is incredibly high, and it can chain devastating Dark and Fire skills. ### 5. Jormuntide Ignis (Fire / Dragon) * **Partner Skill:** *Stormbringer Lava Dragon* (Can be ridden as a water mount. Enhances Fire attacks while mounted). * **Why it's elite:** Outside of the pure Legendaries, its raw attack scaling is legendary. The dual Fire/Dragon typing leaves it with almost no weaknesses, and it applies heavy damage-over-time burns. ### 6. Bellanoir Libero (Dark) * **Partner Skill:** *Nightmare Siren* (Activates a powerful beam attack upon command). * **Why it's elite:** A terrifying spellcaster. Its unique passive trait, *Siren of the Void*, grants a permanent 20% boost to both Dark and Ice attacks, making it a lethal ranged combatant. ### 7. Blazamut (Fire) * **Partner Skill:** *Magma Kaiser* (Can be ridden as a land mount. Enhances Fire attacks while mounted). * **Why it's elite:** A pure offensive juggernaut. It has an incredibly high base attack stat and access to *Volcanic Burst*, one of the hardest-hitting physical Fire moves in the game. ### 8. Shadowbeak (Dark) * **Partner Skill:** *Modified DNA* (Can be ridden as a flying mount. Enhances Dark attacks while mounted). * **Why it's elite:** It possesses the exclusive skill *Divine Disaster*, an incredible move that fires tracking lasers before charging the target, dealing massive multi-hit Dark damage. ### 9. Anubis (Ground) * **Partner Skill:** *Guardian Deity* (Applies Ground damage to the player's attacks while fighting together. Automatically dodges attacks with a sidestep). * **Why it's elite:** It is incredibly difficult for AI bosses to hit because of its unique built-in evasion loop. It mixes high speed with heavy-hitting Ground skills like *Ground Smash*. ### 10. Knocklem (Ground) * **Partner Skill:** *Steel Guardian* (When activated, attacks targets with a massive fist while greatly boosting its own attack and defense). * **Why it's elite:** When its partner skill is active, its stats spike dramatically, making it a temporary S-tier powerhouse that can solo high-level raid bosses.
